# Quiet Steps — Part 3.1

Quiet Steps is a proposed mobile skills-practice prototype for the course's Research and Design milestone. It is not a working mental-health service, therapy product, diagnosis tool, or emergency-support system.

## Current milestone

The Part 3.1 deliverable is a Figma design plus supporting rationale and evidence. A working application is intentionally out of scope for this milestone.

## Proposed experience

People choose either a short paced-breathing practice or a STOP skill. Check-in is optional, leaving is always allowed, and crisis support stays visible. See [docs/design.md](docs/design.md) for the full flow, safety boundaries, model role, data plan, and research/design mapping.
